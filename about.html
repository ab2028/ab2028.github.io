About

I'm a high school student interested in mathematics. In 2026, I attended the Ross Mathematics Program in Ohio, where I spent six weeks studying proof-based number theory. I have also taken courses in statistics, calculus 3, real analysis, and linear algebra. This site collects some of my mathematical writing, publications, and other notes. It's still a work in progress, and more resources will be added over time.
