Critical Points of Linear Self-Attention with Quadratic Loss on a Single Training Example
Ava Berenji, Khang Nguyen, Guido Montufar
OPT 2026 Workshop at NeurIPS, Accepted Poster, December 2026 · OpenReview

We study the optimization landscape of linear self-attention, a simplified version of the attention mechanism used in transformers. For a single-head, single-layer model trained on one example with quadratic loss, we completely characterize the critical points (where the gradient of the loss vanishes). Our approach reduces the problem to a previously studied optimization problem for deep linear networks, addresses an error in the existing proof, and then lifts the resulting critical points back to the original parametrization using the singular value decomposition of the input matrix. We are currently revising the manuscript for the camera-ready version.
