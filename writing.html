A Note on Theorems 1 and 3 of Zhou and Liang (2018)
September 2026

Zhou and Liang’s ICLR 2018 paper, Critical Points of Linear Neural Networks: Analytical Forms and Landscape Properties, characterizes the critical points of deep linear neural networks. This note corrects a pseudoinverse step in the proofs of Theorems 1 and 3 and gives an alternative argument recovering their parametrizations.

A Companion to Serre’s A Course in Arithmetic
September 2026 · In progress

I started writing this while working through Serre’s A Course in Arithmetic. Serre is famously concise, and I kept having to fill in background knowledge or skipped steps as I read. This text (still a work in progress) records some of my process for other readers working through Chapters I–IV.
